Dealer rotates each round. If all players stay or bust, the round ends automatically.

Official Play Notes

  • Stayed players can still be targeted by action cards.
  • Busted players are out immediately and score 0 for the round.
  • Busted cards stay face down in front of the player until round end.
  • If the deck runs out mid-round, shuffle only the discard pile back into the deck.
  • In Vengeance, Zero means you score 0 unless you reach Flip 7, so the app recommends HIT while Zero is active and Flip 7 is not complete.
