Official Play Notes

  • On your turn, choose HIT or STAY.
  • Staying does not protect cards; actions can still target you.
  • If you bust, you are out immediately and score 0 for the round.
  • Busted cards stay face down in front of the player and out of play.
  • Cards in front of players, including busted cards, are not discarded until round end.
  • If the deck runs out mid-round, shuffle only the discard pile back into the deck.
