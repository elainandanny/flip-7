Rules

  • Stayed players can still be targeted by action cards.
  • Busted players are out immediately and score 0 for the round.
  • Busted cards stay visible but greyed out until round end.
  • If the deck runs out mid-round, shuffle only the discard pile back into the deck.
  • In Vengeance, Zero scores 0 unless the player reaches Flip 7.
  • Vengeance modifiers: subtract first, then ÷2.
  • Flip 7 bonus: +15 points (both versions).
  • Swap: the action owner swaps one of their cards with one card from another non-busted player.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • H — Hit
  • S — Stay
  • Esc — Close modal / zoom
